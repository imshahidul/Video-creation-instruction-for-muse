  renumber from Day 1, remove source branding, keep structure + lesson.

  Brand Beats says-". NEVER বল্টু দি গ্রেট স্যার / Professor Max.

  assigns. Exceptions recorded per video (threshold-gap kept Day 3 by user
  decision; seal Bangla posted as Day 2, single-option-aversion also Day 2
  by user decision after the duplicate was flagged).

  from line one, failure as dialogue, turn with "পরেরদিন", "আর মাত্র",
  "করো না". New character distinct from রফিক/করিম/জামাল চাচা.
- **Item-match, location, detailed B-roll** rules apply to every build.
- **No dash characters** in scripts/drafts (checked before presentation).
- **No-copied-names** checked (banned grep zero) before delivery.
- **English versions:** localized script (Uncle Jack / Uncle Ray / Uncle
  Joe, dollars, holiday season, "The CEO of Brand Beats says"), Melody

  Posted to LinkedIn + Pinterest on user order (never auto-posted).
